HOW TO DEPLOY MY TOY RESTAURANT (Simple Explanation!)
======================================================

Imagine we are opening a digital toy restaurant. To run this restaurant, we have three main parts:

1. THE MENU BOARD (Frontend):
   - This is the beautiful display board with pictures of food at the entrance. Customers tap it to choose what they want.
   
2. THE KITCHEN & CHEF (Backend):
   - The chef stands in the kitchen, listens to orders from the Menu Board, talks to the database, and cooks the food.

3. THE RECIPE & HISTORY NOTEBOOK (Database):
   - A big book locked in a safe that remembers all the menu items, prices, and who ordered what.

------------------------------------------------------
WHERE TO PUT EACH PART (The Hosting Services)
------------------------------------------------------

* VERCEL OR NETLIFY (For the Menu Board / Frontend):
  - These are super-fast bulletin boards. They are perfect for showing pictures and buttons to customers.
  - We connect our frontend code to Vercel/Netlify, and they give us a web link (like my-menu.vercel.app) that anyone in the world can click on.

* RENDER OR RAILWAY (For the Kitchen / Backend):
  - These are powerful houses with stoves and electricity that stay on all day and night.
  - We connect our backend code, tell the chef the secret recipes by typing in environment variables (like the path to our Database and Cloudinary picture safe), and they run our server continuously.

* SUPABASE OR NEON (For the Recipe Notebook / Database):
  - These are cloud safes. They store all of our data in rows and columns and never forget it.
  - We create a project, get a secret key (called DATABASE_URL), and hand it to our Kitchen (Render/Railway) so the chef can open the safe.

* FIREBASE:
  - A giant toy box from Google that has its own menu board, chef, and notebook all in one place. We do not need it because we already built a custom SQL database and custom Express server.

------------------------------------------------------
EASY 1-2-3 METHOD TO DEPLOY EVERYTHING
------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: Create the Recipe Safe (Database)
- Go to Neon.tech or Supabase.com, sign up, and create a database.
- Copy the database link (DATABASE_URL).

STEP 2: Hire the Chef (Backend on Render)
- Go to Render.com and choose "Web Service".
- Connect your backend code.
- Paste the database link you copied in Step 1, along with your JWT secret and Cloudinary keys into the "Environment Variables" section.
- Render will start the backend and give you a backend URL (like https://my-kitchen.onrender.com).

STEP 3: Hang the Menu Board (Frontend on Vercel)
- Go to Vercel.com and import your frontend project.
- In the settings, create an environment variable called VITE_API_URL and paste your backend URL from Step 2.
- Click "Deploy". Vercel will give you your final website link!
